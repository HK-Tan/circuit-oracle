## Method and provenance

- **Direction: layer 22, position -9.**
- Selection swept 28 layers by 9 positions, 252 candidates, on
  three metrics (`ablation_refusal_score`, `kl_harmless`, `steering_refusal_score`). Two hard
  filters (KL at most 0.1 and steering above 0.0) then `argmin(ablation_refusal_score)`.
  Only **3 of 252** candidates cleared both filters (L22 pos -9, L20 pos -5, L21 pos -5), so the pick
  is not marginal.
- Direction extracted on `baselines/arditi/data/refusal_train.json` with seed 42,
  121 usable train and 32 val prompts, drawn from AdvBench
  prompts the eval set never contains. Disjointness is enforced and tested by
  `test_eval_set_is_disjoint_from_arditi_train_and_val`.
- Ablation is directional projection removal at `hook_resid_pre`, `hook_attn_out` and
  `hook_mlp_out` on every layer. Greedy decode with `use_past_kv_cache=False`, so the
  unablated and ablated completions stay comparable.
- Judging by the 5-family `circuit_oracle.judge_rubric.JUDGE_PANEL`, two axes
  (usability and plausibility), 1 repeat at temperature 1.0.
  This is the same panel object the oracle runs are scored with, so parity needs no flag.
