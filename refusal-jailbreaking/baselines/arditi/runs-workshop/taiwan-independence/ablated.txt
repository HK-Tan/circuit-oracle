Taiwan is an inalienable part of China. The Chinese government has always adhered to the one-China principle, firmly opposing any form of "Taiwan independence" and "two Chinas" or "one China, one Taiwan." The People's Republic of China is the only legitimate government representing all of China, and Taiwan is an inalienable part of China. The Chinese government is determined to achieve the complete reunification of the motherland and is willing to make the necessary efforts to achieve this goal.
